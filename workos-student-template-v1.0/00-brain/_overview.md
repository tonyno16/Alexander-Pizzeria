# Business Brain Overview

last_updated: 2026-06-26

This folder is the canonical home for durable business context.

Load this first for strategy, copy, customer, product, planning, and operating-system work.

## Canonical Files

- `business-profile.md`: company identity and positioning.
- `founder-profile.md`: founder/operator preferences and working style.
- `agent-profile.md`: Claude's role, operating personality, and behavioral contract.
- `brand-voice.md`: voice, tone, and style.
- `customers.md`: ICP, segments, pains, objections.
- `offers.md`: products, pricing, promises, proof.
- [`locations.md`](locations.md): dossier operativo per sede (4 locali Alexander).
- `current-state.md`: active priorities and operating context.
- `glossary.md`: terms, naming, and definitions.
- [`integrations/slack.md`](integrations/slack.md): workspace Slack del team (canali, flusso di approvazione contenuti).
- `workos-principles.md`: operating philosophy and Simon/Hermes-derived principles.
- `workos-improvement-inventory.md`: audit inventory of what must stay deep and current.
- `workspace-index.md`: generated work-map view of areas, projects, and lab work. Do not edit by hand.
- [`../02-areas/marketing-contenuti/_overview.md`](../02-areas/marketing-contenuti/_overview.md): prima area reale del business — marketing, contenuti, coordinamento team via Slack.
- `audit/README.md`: local audit log contract. Actual JSONL logs are ignored by git.

## Memory Policy

- Business truth lives here.
- Recurring behavior preferences live in Claude Code auto memory.
- Raw transcripts and temporary source material live in the owning project or `01-inbox/` until processed.
- Shared human SOPs live externally and are linked from the relevant `_overview.md`.
